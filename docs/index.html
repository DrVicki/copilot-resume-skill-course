The setup problem

A prompt gives you one output. A system keeps the standard.

A blank chat forgets the evidence rules, review standard, and output structure you established last time. Quality drift is not an AI mystery; it is a missing operating system.

“Select from verified evidence. Never write around a genuine gap.”

Non-negotiables

Evidence before polish.

The workflow is designed to improve fit without inventing experience or hiding uncertainty.

  • ✓

    Every claim needs a source. Career evidence or unchanged resume facts—not inference.

  • ✓

    Every gap stays visible. Missing experience is a decision point, not a writing opportunity.

  • ✓

    Every draft ends with review. Copilot assists; the student verifies and decides.
